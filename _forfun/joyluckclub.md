Mahjong is a strategic, four-player tile-based game of skill and calculation that originated in China during the 19th-century Qing Dynasty. As a committee member, I host biweekly Mahjong nights at Newnham College, creating a friendly and inclusive environment for players of all skill levels to practise and socialise.
