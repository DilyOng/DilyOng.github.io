I organised and hosted a Moon Observation Night at the University of Cambridge's Institute of Astronomy, bringing together a group of attendees for an evening that combined outreach and hands-on observing. The event began with a guided tour introducing telescope models and astronomy fun facts, before moving to the dome observatory for live viewing of the full Moon and Jupiter through a 16-inch professional telescope. To ensure the session ran smoothly and responsibly, I set up a registration process, coordinated logistics and communications, shared clear meeting-point guidance, and established safety rules to protect the University's equipment while creating a welcoming experience for participants.
